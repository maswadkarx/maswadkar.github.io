Krishi AI is where my farming roots and my work in artificial intelligence meet. It is a bootstrapped, independently built product designed to make practical agricultural knowledge more accessible to Indian farmers in their own context and language.

## What it brings together

The Android app combines AI chat, plant diagnosis, live mandi prices sourced from India’s Open Government Data platform, local weather, multilingual voice conversations, crop knowledge, saved history, and conversation export.

The product spans Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android, Firebase and cloud functions on the backend, and a companion web experience. I handle product direction, application development, backend, infrastructure, releases, and the learning loop with users.

## From an old idea to an AI-native product

The idea has a longer history. An earlier app, DigitalMandi, reached 41,000 users in 2017 before I stopped maintaining it. Krishi AI is both a return to that mission and a more ambitious rebuild: broader guidance, richer language access, and AI used as a practical interface to agricultural knowledge.

## Current public milestones

- More than 10,000 Google Play downloads as of July 2026.
- Accepted into the Google for Startups Cloud Program.
- A strategic partnership with Headway Earth to bring Krishi AI’s AgriTech services to customers in Nepal.
- Shared publicly through a first talk, *From Village Fields to AI: Building Krishi AI with LLM Tools*.

The product is still evolving. The purpose remains steady: help farmers get more timely access to knowledge, process guidance, pricing, and support.
