DigitalMandi was an early attempt to make agricultural market information easier to reach on a phone. In 2017, the app grew to 41,000 users.

Then life changed, priorities moved, and I stopped maintaining it. Without updates or stewardship, usage slowly fell to zero.

## The lesson that stayed

The experience left me with a simple principle: show up. Products need care after launch, not only energy before it. When I returned to the problem years later with Krishi AI, that lesson shaped how I thought about releases, feedback, and the long work of earning usefulness.

DigitalMandi is archived, but its mission did not disappear. It became part of the foundation for what came next.
