DigitalMandi was my first mobile attempt to make agricultural market information easier for farmers to reach. The Android app grew to 41,000 users in 2017, then declined after I stopped maintaining it. Its most durable outcome was a lesson in product stewardship that now shapes Krishi AI.

## Problem and intended users

Agricultural prices can be difficult to discover at the moment a farmer needs them. DigitalMandi was intended to put useful market information on a phone in a simpler, more direct form.

## My role and contribution

I conceived, funded, built, launched, and initially operated the app independently. I was also responsible for the decision not to continue maintaining it when life and priorities changed.

## Evidence and outcome

I documented the app’s package name, 41,000-user milestone, and later decline in a [public retrospective on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/maswadkar_krishiai-startupjourney-buildinpublic-activity-7444594338807873537-vm5y). The product is no longer available as an actively maintained service, and I do not present the historical user count as current usage.

## The lesson that stayed

The experience left me with a simple principle: show up. Products need care after launch, not only energy before it. When I returned to the problem years later with Krishi AI, that lesson shaped how I thought about releases, feedback, and the long work of earning usefulness.

## Limitations and status

DigitalMandi is archived. There is no current product link or public source repository to offer here, and the case study is a retrospective rather than a claim that the service is still operating. The mission did not disappear; it became part of the foundation for what came next.
