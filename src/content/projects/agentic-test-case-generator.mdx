The Agentic Test Case Generator connects two parts of my career: deep experience in quality engineering and newer work with AI agents.

## The workflow

Users can upload requirements from Word, Markdown, or Excel, or import work from Jira and Azure DevOps. The system extracts requirements, enriches them with grounded context from supporting links and artifacts, generates test cases into a user-defined template, and exposes coverage diagnostics before anything is approved.

Approved candidates can be previewed for browser-automation readiness and executed with Playwright. Outputs can be exported as CSV, Excel, or JSON.

## Human control is part of the design

This is not a black-box test generator. Review, approval, security boundaries, artifact retention, integration credentials, and production authentication are documented parts of the system. The goal is to remove repetition while preserving the judgment that good testing depends on.
