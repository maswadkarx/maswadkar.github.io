This portfolio is a static, chapter-led public home for my work, writing, current direction, résumé, media, and verified public profiles. It is designed to give people—and standards-compliant search and AI crawlers—one canonical place to understand the whole story.

## The problem

A résumé is useful, but it compresses a person into titles and dates. A link page connects destinations, but it rarely explains why those destinations belong together. This project is designed to connect the work, the thinking, the direction, and the life around them.

## The approach

The homepage uses five chapters—Making, Thinking, Becoming, Living, and Connecting. Each chapter offers a quick, memorable entry point while conventional detail pages keep the content easy to browse, link, and find through search.

The content system is intentionally truth-first. Draft entries stay out of production, optional sections disappear when their source material is missing, and generated imagery is treated as editorial atmosphere rather than documentary evidence.

I directed and implemented the site in Astro with typed content collections and static HTML routes. The discoverability layer includes canonical URLs, public sitemaps with content-based modification dates, RSS, structured data, social metadata, a human-readable `llms.txt`, and a crawler policy that allows legitimate public indexing.

## The content pass

The portfolio now brings together public work, first-party writing, a professional timeline, current direction, media, and verified channels. Source material is drawn from public profiles and project repositories; uncertain details remain unpublished until they can be confirmed.

The [public repository](https://github.com/maswadkarx/maswadkarx.github.io) contains the site and its deployment configuration. Automated checks verify metadata, sitemap coverage, structured data, internal links, the 404 response, and public discovery files before deployment.

## Limitations and current status

This is an evolving personal publication, not a CMS or a complete autobiography. It intentionally has no visitor analytics, newsletter, contact-form backend, or fabricated placeholder content. Search indexing, rankings, and AI citations are controlled by external systems and cannot be guaranteed; the site can only make its content accessible, consistent, and verifiable.
