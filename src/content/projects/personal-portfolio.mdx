This site began with a simple requirement: make one public place broad enough to hold a professional portfolio and human enough to feel like a real person lives behind it.

## The problem

A résumé is useful, but it compresses a person into titles and dates. A link page connects destinations, but it rarely explains why those destinations belong together. This project is designed to connect the work, the thinking, the direction, and the life around them.

## The approach

The homepage uses five chapters—Making, Thinking, Becoming, Living, and Connecting. Each chapter offers a quick, memorable entry point while conventional detail pages keep the content easy to browse, link, and find through search.

The content system is intentionally truth-first. Draft entries stay out of production, optional sections disappear when their source material is missing, and generated imagery is treated as editorial atmosphere rather than documentary evidence.

## The content pass

The portfolio now brings together public work, writing, a professional timeline, current direction, and verified channels. Source material is drawn from public profiles and project repositories; uncertain details remain unpublished until they can be confirmed.
