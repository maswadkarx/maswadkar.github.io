This eight-minute product walkthrough shows how Krishi AI brings agricultural guidance, plant diagnosis, and live market prices into one multilingual Android experience for Indian farmers.

## What the walkthrough shows

The recording moves through the farmer-facing product rather than presenting only a slide deck. It demonstrates the main paths described in the public application listing:

- AI chat for questions about crops, soil, pests, fertilisers, and farming processes.
- Plant diagnosis from an image, with AI used to help interpret the visible problem.
- Live mandi prices sourced from India’s Open Government Data platform.
- Local-language access intended to make the experience more useful across different regions.
