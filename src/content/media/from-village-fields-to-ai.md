This 19-minute talk explains how I returned to an old agricultural product idea and built Krishi AI with a new generation of LLM-assisted tools. It is a practical account of what AI accelerated—and what still required product judgment, domain context, testing, and persistence.

## What the talk answers

The talk connects three parts of my story: growing up in a farming family, building the earlier DigitalMandi app, and later working across software delivery, cloud, machine learning, and LLM systems. Krishi AI became the point where those threads met.

I describe how LLM tools supported research, ideation, architecture, coding, and debugging while I developed a real Android product. The central lesson is not that AI removes the difficult work. It changes the build loop: ideas can be tested faster, but the builder still has to understand the user, evaluate the result, fix failures, and keep the product alive after the first release.

## Product themes covered

- Moving from a historical farming-information app to a maintained AI-native product.
- Combining AI chat, plant diagnosis, mandi prices, agricultural knowledge, and multilingual access.
- Using LLMs across the product-development workflow rather than only as a user-facing chat feature.
- The practical gap between a convincing prototype and a dependable public application.
- Why farming context and continued user learning matter alongside technical capability.
