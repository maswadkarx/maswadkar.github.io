In the India of my childhood, the kid who brought the cricket bat and ball had an unusual kind of power: access to the scarce tools could decide who played and who made the rules.

Software creation is moving into a different economy. AI coding tools make implementation faster and more available, shifting attention away from possession of the tool and toward judgment, ideas, problem selection, and what we choose to build.
