Cost optimisation can produce the opposite of its intended outcome when a metric becomes the target and the wider system is ignored. The article uses the Cobra Effect as a way to think about LLM adoption, incentives, and the difference between reducing one bill and creating lasting value.

The practical question is not simply whether an AI system is cheaper. It is whether the complete workflow—quality, review, correction, risk, infrastructure, and human time included—has improved.
