Most profile sites flatten a person into a sequence of roles. That can be useful, but it is not the whole story.

I want this site to become one place for the things I choose to tell the world: projects, a résumé, writing, aspirations, hobbies, and the public channels where those ideas continue. Not everything needs to appear at once. What matters is that every piece belongs to the same throughline.

## A portfolio that can breathe

The work needs enough space to be understood. A project page should explain the problem, the choices, and what changed—not only show a logo or a technology list.

The writing needs a permanent home too. Social posts move quickly; a personal site can keep the ideas that deserve more time.

## The human part matters

Professional credibility and personality are not opposites. The interests, questions, and ambitions around the work often explain how that work came to be.

This first version deliberately leaves unverified details out. The site will become more complete as the real source material arrives, while staying honest at every stage.
