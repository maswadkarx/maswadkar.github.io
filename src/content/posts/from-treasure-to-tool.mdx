In *Jurassic Park*, Dr. Alan Grant’s treasured fossil claw loses its old significance when he encounters living dinosaurs. The object was not worthless; the world around it had changed.

The essay uses that moment to examine software development in the age of AI coding assistants. Individual lines of code still matter, but the more durable skill moves toward understanding the problem, shaping the architecture, guiding the tools, integrating the result, and validating what was produced.
