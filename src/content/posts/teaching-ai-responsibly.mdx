The essay uses a classroom exchange to explain the relationship between a broad language model and a smaller, specialised one. The teacher holds wider experience; the student grows through focused guidance and curated learning.

The analogy opens a practical argument for targeted models: narrower systems can become highly useful in a defined domain when the learning material and guidance are chosen with care.
